TWENTY-TWO IDEAS
PRECISION
1 · Precision loupeiOS magnifier · Resolve dual timelineA zoom bubble opens over any edge drag — frame ticks, snap targets, media limits — so you never zoom the whole timeline to land one frame.AI: inspect(t) returns the loupe's contents as JSON — the context an agent reads before proposing an edit.try: drag any clip edge
2 · Fine drag — pull awayiOS scrubber gearingMid-drag, pull the pointer away from the track: distance gears the drag down smoothly to 1/10× and the loupe opens. No keys — precision is how far you pull. (⇧ still forces fine.)AI: agents are frame-exact natively — this gives humans parity with them.try: drag an edge, then drift downward
3 · Headroom & hold-framestudio's overrun, evolvedDrag past either end of the footage: the edge sticks at the media wall, then breaks through with a thud — the extra frames hold the first/last frame, hatched and labeled ❚❚. The loupe draws the bracket; the monitor shows the frozen frame.AI: media limits in inspect() stop impossible-trim proposals; «media limit» becomes a structured refusal.try: drag a scene's in-point left past its footage
MAGNETISM
4 · Sticky ends + breakawayKeynote haptic guidesSnap has hysteresis: edges lock on, hold through small wobbles, then release with a pop past escape distance. Sticky, never grabby.AI: snap targets double as anchors — «align cta.in to hero.out», no frame math, re-resolved at apply time.try: drag a clip slowly across another's end
5 · Named snap targetsfirst principlesThe HUD names what you snapped to — «hero out», «beat 29», «playhead» — so alignment is a fact, not a guess.AI: the names ARE the addressing scheme: hero.out, beat:29, marker:beat-drop, playhead.try: watch the HUD while snapped
6 · Beat gridCapCut auto-beat · Ableton gridThe music track publishes its beats to the ruler; every edit can land on the pulse. Downbeats weigh more.AI: beats as data make «cut on the downbeats» a trivial, verifiable instruction.try: drag an SFX near the beat dots
ELASTIC TIME
7 · Ripple insert (＋TIME)Logic Insert Silence · Pro Tools shuffleDrag a clip's ✂ strip sideways to tear the timeline open — or drag the ＋ pill at any junction (click = +0.5s), or press R for the armed mode. Everything downstream shifts live and the render window grows; drag left removes time. Straddling clips split and stay in sync; music beds flow through and grow.AI: one atomic insertTime(at, dur) verb with a dry-run plan — no 15-attribute rewrite arithmetic.try: drag the ✂ strip in the middle of HERO
8 · Gaps are objectsFCPX gap clipsHover a gap → duration chip: × closes it (ripple delete), ≈ bridges a split's two halves back into one flowing clip. On magnetic tracks inserted time becomes a dashed gap clip you can trim or delete.AI: gaps enumerable («any dead air?») and reservable — insert a gap for a shot you're about to generate.try: R-insert into SCENES, then hover the gap
9 · Ripple trimFCPX · PremiereOn magnetic tracks edge trims ripple automatically — downstream follows live. On free tracks, ⌘-drag an edge to ripple, or grab the lower half of the handle.AI: an intent verb — change pacing, downstream sync preserved by construction.try: trim a scene edge and watch downstream
10 · Roll at junctionsPremiere · Avid rollersHover where two clips meet: a roll handle appears; drag moves the boundary — one grows, one shrinks, nothing downstream moves.AI: intent verb — move the cut point, total length invariant. Agents pick verbs by intent, not math.try: hover a junction between scenes
11 · Slip in placePremiere slip toolDrag the ⇄ pill that appears mid-clip: the source slides under fixed in/out — the filmstrip moves, the loupe flips to source view. (⌥-drag anywhere on the body also works.)AI: intent verb — change which moment plays, placement untouched. Ideal for retiming generated takes.try: hover a scene, drag its ⇄ pill
STRUCTURE
12 · Magnetic storyline, per trackFCPX magnetic timelineMAGNET on a track means no gaps, no overlaps — drag to reorder, neighbors part and repack with a spring. Off = free staging, like today.AI: agents reorder a list («move cta before social») instead of computing frames — packing makes overlap bugs impossible.try: drag a scene left/right to reorder · toggle MAGNET
13 · Connected clipsFCPX connectionsCaptions, lower-thirds and SFX connect to the scene they annotate (stem at their left edge). Move or ripple the scene — they ride along. Sync survives editing.AI: relationships beat offsets — connect(caption, hero) survives every later edit without re-computation.try: drag HERO and watch its lower-third
14 · Blade with previewCapCut split · Avid Smart Tool zonesEvery clip has a ✂ strip along its lower edge: hovering shows the cut line and tints the doomed half, click cuts — no mode. The playhead cluster's ✂ splits every track; B still arms the classic blade; right-click offers both. Cuts snap to beats and the playhead.AI: split(clip, at anchor) — cut points addressed by beat/marker/playhead, not frame numbers.try: hover a clip's lower edge, click
NAVIGATION
15 · MinimapResolve cut-page upper timelineA constant overview strip: drag the window to pan, drag its edges to zoom, click to jump. You always know where you are.AI: what it draws is the compact timeline summary an agent reads as context.try: the strip above the ruler
16 · Zoom that never loses youAbleton Z⌘-scroll zooms around the cursor; Z fits the selection, F fits everything; playback page-follows. Position is never the cost of precision.AI: humans only — agents don't scroll. Honest non-fit.try: ⌘-scroll, then Z on a selected clip
17 · SkimmingFCPX skimmerWith SKIM on, hovering the ruler previews that frame in the monitor without moving the playhead. Looking is free; the playhead is a decision.AI: the agent analog is frame sampling — render any t without touching playback state.try: hover the ruler slowly
FEEDBACK & FLOW
18 · One delta HUDPremiere's numbers, unifiedEvery temporal op speaks one grammar: Δ frames + seconds, new in/out/duration, snap target name. Move, trim, slip, ripple — same voice.AI: every commit returns {op, Δ, targets, affected} — the structured receipt an agent uses to verify its own edit.try: any drag
19 · One grammar — clicks and keysResolve top/tail · Avid Smart ToolEvery command has a pointer path: hover the playhead for ⇤ ✂ ⇥ (with previews of exactly what they'll remove), a selection bar with ◂1f 1f▸ steppers, double-click the ruler for markers, right-click for the rest. Keys ( , . [ ] B R M ) are accelerators, never requirements.AI: keys, clicks, and API calls are three skins on the same verbs.try: hover the playhead in the ruler · select a clip
20 · Physics, sound, named undoApple polish everywhereClips settle with a spring, snaps tick, cuts click, ripples whoosh — and every commit posts a named toast («Rippled +0.8s») with ⌘Z always one step away.AI: labeled transactions = an audit trail — agent edits show up as the same named toasts, revertable by id.try: drop anything · ⌘Z
BONUS · GENERATIVE
21 · Take stacksFCPX auditions × FrameDiff takesA generative clip holds its alternate takes in one slot. Cycle the pinned take in place — different durations ripple the storyline automatically. Auditioning is an edit, not a detour.AI: agents fill the stack, a judge (human or model) pins — ripple handles the layout consequences.try: click the t2 badge on HERO
BONUS · ORDER
22 · Layers & stackinglinked lists all the way downEvery element has an explicit order: layers form a draggable list (upper covers lower), and within a layer clips keep their own stacking order.
